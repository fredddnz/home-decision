    About this preview

    This is a local, interactive MVP. “Example address, Auckland” uses synthetic information. Real addresses trigger LINZ, Council flood and dated Council rating valuation checks, followed by six source-bound AI analysis roles. Open a category or purchase support status for results.

    Purchase support is a decision score, not a success probability. Council CV is a dated rating valuation, not a current market estimate or recorded sale. Missing comparable sales, building, legal and finance evidence keeps support unavailable; AI cannot invent the missing utilities.

    Only address and geometry go to public data providers. Relevant source excerpts, expected price and your notes are sent to OpenAI through the locally signed-in Codex CLI; this uses your existing Codex quota. Raw files are not uploaded. The named demo makes no model calls. Saved projects expire after 30 days.
